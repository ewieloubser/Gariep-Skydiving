Good to know

Age & consent

Minimum age 14. Jumpers aged 14–17 must have a parent or guardian sign consent on the day. Bring ID.

Weight limit

Maximum 100 kg per jumper, for the safety of the tandem harness and canopy. Please be accurate.

Arrive 40 min early

Get to the skydiving desk at least 40 minutes before your window for check-in, indemnity, briefing and kit-up.

Payment

R4 890 per tandem jump. Pay in full or a 50% deposit now, balance before the event. All payments non-refundable.

Weather

Skydiving is weather-dependent. If jumps are grounded by conditions on the day, the operator will arrange an alternative window.

Operated by

Tandem jumps are run by an independent SACAA Part 105 approved parachuting operator at Big Water Airport (FAGA).
